◆ Demo visualization — city positions are real geographic coordinates; dealer counts and price-vs-MSRP figures are sample data for design preview. Current weather (temperature, condition and day/night) is live from Open-Meteo (free, no key) and labelled with its fetch time; if the request fails it falls back to clearly-marked sample weather. In production each pin is a real dealership geocoded from LotCheck's locations pipeline (get-dealer-sentiment); each city's price-vs-MSRP is computed from scraped listings measured against msrp_catalog; and weather is read live from Open-Meteo. Terrain shading is stylized, not survey elevation.
