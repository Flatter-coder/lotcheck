Does trust follow through on price?

Left rail ranks every dealer by Google review score; right rail ranks the same dealers by price position vs MSRP. Each line ties a dealer's reputation to what they actually charge. A line that stays high on both ends — flat across the top — is the honest sweet spot. Lines that plunge are well-liked but pricey, or cheap but poorly rated.
