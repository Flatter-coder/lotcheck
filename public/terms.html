Plain-language summary. LotCheck is built for Alberta and is offered only to people located in Alberta. Our reports apply Alberta’s rules — AMVIC all-in advertising, Alberta EVAP rebates, the AMVIC dealer registry, Alberta fee benchmarks. Running one for a vehicle or buyer in another province would give you Alberta answers to a different province’s question, which is worse than giving you nothing.

1. Where LotCheck is offered

LotCheck is offered only to users located in Alberta, Canada. By using LotCheck you represent that you are located in Alberta. Accessing or using the service from outside Alberta — including from British Columbia, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Quebec, the Atlantic provinces and the territories, or from outside Canada — is a breach of these Terms.

We use network-level location signals to determine where a request comes from. Those signals are imperfect. Canadian carriers route traffic through regional hubs, so an Alberta user can appear to be elsewhere. If that happens to you, you may confirm that you are in Alberta and continue. That confirmation is a representation you are making to us, and these Terms apply to it.

We may add provinces or other jurisdictions over time. Until we do, a report for a vehicle or buyer outside Alberta is outside what this service is built to answer.

2. What LotCheck is, and is not

LotCheck is an information service. It reads publicly available information — dealers’ own advertised listings, manufacturers’ published prices, and government registries — and reports what it finds, with the source for each figure.

LotCheck is not:

  • a motor vehicle dealer, broker, or salesperson;
  • legal, financial, tax or investment advice;
  • a warranty, guarantee, or inspection of any vehicle;
  • a statement that any dealer has done anything unlawful.

A manufacturer’s MSRP is a suggested price. A dealer is free to set its own selling price. Where a report shows a difference between an advertised price and a manufacturer’s published figure, that is arithmetic and provenance — not an allegation.

3. Accuracy, and its limits

We show the source and basis for every figure so you can check it yourself, and we decline to make a comparison when we cannot make it soundly. Published prices change, listings change, and a catalogue entry prices a trim while the vehicle in front of you may carry options. Confirm anything that matters with the dealer and the manufacturer before you rely on it.
