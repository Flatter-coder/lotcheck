Local reviews vs MSRP reputation × price · live

The index says where prices sit; it doesn't say who to trust. Which local dealers are both the cheapest and the highest-rated? Every dealership is plotted by its Google review score against how far its listings sit above or below MSRP — the bottom-right is below MSRP and higher-rated.

Each dot is a dealership: Google review score left→right (1.0–5.0★), above / below MSRP top→bottom, dot size ≈ inventory. Hover any dot for the full card; between hovers the dots drift as fresh ratings land — motion that's simulated for this preview.

In production: the LMI is computed from LotCheck's verified msrp_catalog (29 makes) on the catalog's real daily cadence; markup % comes from real scraped dealer listings compared against msrp_catalog; dealer ratings & reviews come from the app's real Google reviews pipeline (get-dealer-sentiment); and the pricing-mention rate is genuine AI analysis of review text ("fees", "pricing"). The Reviews vs MSRP is drawn from the same real inputs — each dealer's Google review score & count from get-dealer-sentiment plotted against its scraped-listing markup vs msrp_catalog; only the drifting motion is a simulated review stream for this preview. None of it is fabricated. Dealer names shown here are fictional placeholders and are not attached to any real business's rating data.
